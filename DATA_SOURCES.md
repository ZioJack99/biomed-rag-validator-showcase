# Data and method sources

[SciFact](https://github.com/allenai/scifact) and [dataset format](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/doc/data.md): scientific claim/evidence evaluation by Wadden et al. (2020), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974).

[SciFact licensing](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md): claim/evidence annotations CC BY 4.0; abstracts under Open Data Commons Attribution terms.

[cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-small model card](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-small): SNLI/MultiNLI pretrained encoder, Apache-2.0. The encoder weights are not included here.

Only aggregate evaluation summaries and original presentation figures are included here. No raw datasets, trained weights or third-party source code are redistributed. Underlying data/software retain their own terms.
